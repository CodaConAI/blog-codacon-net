We started CodaCon to bridge the gap between what AI can do and what teams actually ship. The technology is moving fast, but most organizations are still figuring out where it fits — and where it doesn't.

This blog is where we'll share what we learn: practical insights from real projects, honest takes on what works, and the occasional deep dive into the tools and techniques that make AI-assisted development productive rather than just impressive.

We'll cover topics like:

- How to structure codebases so AI tools can actually help
- When to automate and when to keep a human in the loop
- Lessons from deploying AI in regulated industries
- The engineering practices that survive contact with production

No hype, no hand-waving. Just what we've seen work.

If you're building something and want to talk about how AI fits into it, [reach out](https://codacon.net).
